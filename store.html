Steel Tide: store, accounts and privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. This page is plain-language information from the game's maker. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

What you are buying

The store sells Credits, the game's own currency, shown with an anchor sign (for example 6,000⚓). Credits are also earned for free by playing: every battle pays.

Credits buy looks only: paint schemes, helmets, uniforms and accessories in the Hangar. Nothing bought with Credits makes a unit faster, tougher or stronger. Items you buy are kept for good.

Credits have no cash value, cannot be traded or cashed out, and only work in Steel Tide.

Prices and payment

Credit packs are priced in Canadian dollars and always marked CAD. Tax may be added at checkout depending on where you live.

Payments are handled by Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's page and never reach this game or its server. Purchases may need a parent's or guardian's permission.

Refunds and support

If something went wrong with a purchase, write to support@example.com with the email address of your account and the date of the purchase. When a purchase is refunded, the Credits from it are taken back out of the account; if they were already spent, the balance can go below zero until it is earned back by playing.

How accounts work

You can play as a guest: Credits and looks are then saved in this browser, on this computer only.

To buy Credits you sign in with your email address. There is no password: the game emails you a one-time code that works for ten minutes. The first time you sign in, what you earned as a guest moves into your account. After that your Credits and looks follow you to any computer you sign in on.

You can delete your account at any time from the Account screen in the game. That removes your account, Credits and looks from the server for good.

Privacy

For accounts, the game's server stores: your email address, a player ID, your Credits balance, the looks you own and have equipped, your purchases (which pack, when, the amount, and Stripe's reference for it) and the results of your battles (what they paid). It stores nothing else about you. Guests have nothing stored on the server.

This information is used to run your account and the store. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone except the services that make it work: Stripe for payments, an email service to send sign-in codes, and the hosting provider.

If your account is deleted, records of purchases are kept without any link to you, because payment records have to be kept.
